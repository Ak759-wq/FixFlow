Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026 · Applies to fixflow-steel.vercel.app

In short: FixFlow keeps your PG records so that you can prove what happened. Only you can read your records. We don't sell data, show ads or use trackers. You can download everything as a file, and you can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

DataWhy
Name, email, optional phone; or your Google name, email and profile picture if you sign in with GoogleTo create your account and sign you in. With Google we only ask for basic profile and email; we never access your Gmail, Drive or contacts.
PG details: PG name, room, address, owner name and number, rent, deposit, datesTo show your tenancy and write notices.
Move-in photos, their file name, type, size, last-modified time and SHA-256 fingerprint, plus upload and lock timesTo keep tamper-evident proof of the room's condition.
Complaints, attached photos, status history and replies you logTo track issues and show how long they stayed unresolved.
Deductions, audit results, notices and an audit log of your actionsTo check deductions against your evidence and keep an honest record of changes.

AI processing

When you ask FixFlow to analyse something, the relevant text or a reduced copy of the photo is sent to Google's Gemini API through our server to get a category, description or audit. Nothing is sent to AI unless you trigger that step. If AI is unavailable, FixFlow uses simple built-in rules instead.

Where it is stored and who can see it

  • Records and photos are stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage). Sign-in uses Firebase Authentication. The app is served by Vercel.
  • Firebase security rules protect every record: it can only be read by the account that created it.
  • If you enter a PG code, the warden of that PG can see the complaints you file there (your name, room, complaint text and photo) and can update their status. Wardens can't see your photos of the room, deductions, notices or account details. Other residents of that PG see only the title, category and urgency of open complaints (no names), so they can add their support.
  • Photo files are stored at long, random links so that they can be shown in the app and in your reports. Anyone you give a link to can open that image, so share links with care.
  • Your sign-in session is kept in your browser's storage by Firebase. The demo uses session storage only.

Demo mode

The demo shows a fictional tenant. Nothing you do in the demo is saved to our database. AI features in the demo still send the text or photo you provide to Gemini for that one request.

Correction & deletion policy

  • Before locking: you can edit the AI description of a move-in photo or remove the photo.
  • After locking: the photo, its upload and lock times and its SHA-256 fingerprint are read-only. The security rules refuse edits and deletion, including from you. This is what makes the record useful as evidence.
  • Corrections: if something is wrong, add a correction. It is stored as a new dated note next to the photo and the original stays unchanged.
  • Complaint history: status changes, warden notes and replies you log are added as new dated entries and are never overwritten.
  • Audit log: every time you add, lock, correct, audit, finalise, download or export something, an entry is saved with a server timestamp. You can read your log in the app; the rules let nobody edit or delete entries.
  • Account deletion: Privacy & data → Delete my account and data permanently deletes your profile, PG details, photos and photo files, corrections, complaints and their history, deductions, notices, audit log and finally your login. It can't be undone. A minimal deletion marker (your account ID and the time you asked) is kept, because it is what allows locked evidence to be removed.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Privacy & data → Download my data gives you a JSON file of everything tied to your account.
  • Delete: as described above, at any time.
  • Retention: we keep your data until you delete it.

Third parties

Google Firebase (database, file storage, sign-in), Vercel (hosting), Google Gemini (AI) and Google Fonts (typefaces) process data only to provide these functions. We don't sell or share data for advertising.

Not legal advice

FixFlow organises your own evidence and drafts messages. It is not a law firm and its audit results are not legal advice.
