How it works

Three steps from check-in day to full refund

No legal jargon. Just dated evidence you control.

01

Scan at move-in

Photograph the bed, wall, bathroom, wardrobe and floor. Gemini tags pre-existing stains and loose fittings, then you lock the record with a server timestamp.

02

Log problems

Report Wi-Fi, water, food or hygiene issues by voice or text in any language. AI sorts and drafts them, and every open issue counts the days it stays unfixed.

03

Claim your deposit

Enter the owner's deductions. AI checks each one against your locked photos and writes WhatsApp, formal and consumer-helpline notices.
