How it works

Three steps from check-in day to your refund

No legal jargon. Dated evidence that you control.

  1. 01

    Move-in evidence

    Photograph your bedroom, bathroom, walls and furniture. Review each photo, let Gemini describe existing damage, then lock the set. Every file gets a server timestamp and a SHA-256 fingerprint.

  2. 02

    Complaint tracking

    Report issues by voice or text in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Each complaint gets an FF reference ID, a status (Open, In progress, Resolved) and a dated history of every update.

  3. 03

    Deposit protection

    Enter each deduction the owner claims. FixFlow checks it against your locked photos and complaint history, flags unsupported charges, totals what you can dispute and drafts the notice.

Model Tenancy Act, 2021 Proposes that a residential deposit should not exceed two months' rent and be refunded when the landlord takes back the vacant premises, after due deductions. It is a model law: it applies only where your state adopts it. Read section 11 (MoHUA)
National Consumer Helpline Call 1915 (8 AM to 8 PM) or file online to register a grievance about a deficient service. consumerhelpline.gov.in
